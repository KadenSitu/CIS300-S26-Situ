Beginner Workout Comparison

This table helps beginners understand the differences between common workout types so they can choose the routine that fits their goals.

Workout Type Benefits Recommended Duration
Cardio Improves heart health and burns calories 20–30 minutes
Strength Training Builds muscle and increases metabolism 30–45 minutes
Flexibility Improves mobility and reduces injury risk 10–20 minutes

Simple Beginner Exercises

  • Cardio: Brisk walking, light jogging, cycling
  • Strength: Bodyweight squats, wall push-ups, dumbbell rows
  • Flexibility: Hamstring stretch, quad stretch, shoulder rolls

As you continue your fitness journey, try mixing these workout types throughout the week to stay balanced and motivated.
